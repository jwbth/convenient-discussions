# A reboot is a new visit

A reboot starts a new visit, just as a browser reload does: comments stay unread and stay old, while new is recomputed from the visits. Users who never leave some talk pages would otherwise see new comments pile up until the whole page is highlighted, and a reboot isn't different enough in nature from a reload to follow other rules.

## Considered options

- **A reboot continues the visit and keeps new as it is**: new comments accumulate on pages that are never left, and reboots and reloads diverge for no reason in their nature.
