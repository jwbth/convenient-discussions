# Logo's back bubble is a knockout outline

The logo's back bubble shows only its top and right edges, the right one stopping in mid-air, the same knockout idiom as Codex's `cdxIconUserPages`. Its body has the size of the front bubble's body, shifted up and right by two edge widths.

## Considered options

- **Full rectangle** (the hidden edges peeking out beside the front bubble): a truthful outline invites a tail as the next truthful step, and with the bubbles this close the tail is hardly readable.
- **Back bubble with a tail**: unreadable for the same reason.
- **Bubbles apart, each with room of its own, as in `cdxIconSpeechBubbles`**: the back bubble would compete with the smiling front bubble for the focal point, and two smiling bubbles would split it.
